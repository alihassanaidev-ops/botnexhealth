# Essential GoTracker Client Questions

1. What exact GoTracker product, edition, version, and build number do you use?

2. Which clinic locations use GoTracker, do they share one database, and which
   version of the GoTracker Synchronizer or other connector is installed?

3. Which GoTracker features does your team use regularly?

4. Please walk us through your normal appointment process from booking the
   appointment until the patient leaves the clinic.

5. Which appointment reasons, statuses, confirmation fields, and Chair Flow
   states do you use, and what does each one mean to your team?

6. How does your team currently handle appointment confirmations, cancellations,
   rescheduling, no answers, and callback requests?

7. When an appointment is cancelled or rescheduled, which GoTracker fields do
   staff update?

8. How does your team use Chair Flow, and when and by whom is a visit marked
   Completed?

9. How does your team currently handle pre-appointment and post-appointment
   patient calls, and where are the results recorded?

10. Who should we contact for GoTracker access, testing, operational questions,
    and final approval?
